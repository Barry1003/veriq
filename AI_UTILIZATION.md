# AI Utilization

**Project:** Veriq — Institutional Intelligence Platform  
**Team Member:** Ishola Usaamah Afolabi  
**Role:** Frontend Developer & Product Experience

## How I Used AI

I used artificial intelligence as a supporting tool during the development of Veriq in the following ways:

1. **Research:** AI assisted me in researching banking challenges, reviewing relevant information, and organizing findings to inform product decisions.
2. **Figma Design:** AI supported my UI/UX design process, including exploring design ideas, refining layouts, and translating product requirements into clearer Figma screen designs.
3. **Code Auditing:** AI helped me review frontend code for potential issues, inconsistencies, and areas for improvement.
4. **Bug Fixing:** AI assisted with debugging by helping identify possible causes of errors and suggesting fixes that could be checked and applied.

## My Contribution

My responsibility on the Veriq team was **frontend development and product experience**. I worked on planning and implementing the user interface, aligning the frontend with the Figma designs, and reviewing its quality and usability.

AI was used to support research, design, code review, and troubleshooting. It did not replace my responsibility for understanding, checking, and integrating the frontend work.
